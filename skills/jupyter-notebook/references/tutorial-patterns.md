# Tutorial Patterns

Apply this structure when writing teaching content and walkthroughs:

- Audience, prerequisites, and learning goals: identify who the content targets, enumerate what prior knowledge is assumed, and specify what the reader will be capable of doing upon completion.
- Outline: include a brief numbered outline at the top so readers can scan the structure before diving in.
- Step-by-step flow: combine a concise markdown explanation with a small, self-contained code cell and a short description of what the output means.
- Exercises: add at least one exercise that cements the core concept, followed by an answer scaffold in the subsequent cell.
- Pitfalls and extensions: highlight one frequently made mistake along with its remedy, and propose one optional extension for readers who want to explore further.
