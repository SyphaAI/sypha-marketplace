## Instructions
You are being asked to produce a company-wide newsletter update. Your goal is to summarize the past week or month for the entire company in newsletter form. Target approximately 20–25 bullet points. The newsletter will be distributed via Slack and email, so structure it accordingly for both channels.

Aim for the following qualities:
- Rich in links: pull relevant documents from Google Drive, reference prominent Slack messages in announcement channels and from executives, cite company-wide emails, and highlight significant developments.
- Brief and direct: each bullet should generally be no longer than 1–2 sentences.
- Written in first-person plural: write as a member of the company. Most bullets should read "we did this" or "we accomplished that."

## Tools to use
Where you have access to the following tools, use them. If access is unavailable, inform the user that richer outputs would result from granting access.

- Slack: look for posts in high-membership channels with many reactions or heavily threaded replies
- Email: search for executive messages covering company-wide announcements
- Calendar: identify meetings with large attendee lists — All-Hands meetings, major company announcements, etc. Any documents attached to those meetings are strong link candidates.
- Documents: link recently published docs that drew significant attention — company-wide vision documents, upcoming quarter or half-year plans, materials authored by senior executives, etc.
- External press: any references to articles or coverage received in the past week are worth highlighting.

If none of these sources are accessible, ask the user what they want covered. In that case, the primary task is polishing the content and fitting it to this format.

## Sections
The company is large — 1000+ people — with numerous teams and initiatives running in parallel. To keep the newsletter navigable, organize content into sections of related topics. Possible groupings include {product development, go to market, finance}, {recruiting, execution, vision}, or {external news, internal news}. Ensure each major area of the company receives appropriate coverage.

## Prioritization
Prioritize:
- Company-wide impact (not team-specific details)
- Leadership announcements
- Major milestones and achievements
- Information that affects the majority of employees
- External recognition or press coverage

Omit:
- Granular team-level updates (those belong in 3Ps)
- Information relevant only to small groups
- Content that duplicates what has already been communicated
