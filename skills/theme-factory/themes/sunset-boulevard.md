# Sunset Boulevard

A vibrant, warm theme drawn from golden-hour sunsets and well matched to energetic, creative presentation styles.

## Color Palette

- **Burnt Orange**: `#e76f51` - Primary accent color
- **Coral**: `#f4a261` - Secondary warm accent
- **Warm Sand**: `#e9c46a` - Highlighting and backgrounds
- **Deep Purple**: `#264653` - Dark contrast and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Creative pitch decks, marketing presentations, lifestyle brand materials, event promotions, inspirational content.
