# Desert Rose

A refined, understated theme built around dusty, muted tones well suited to polished, elegant presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Dusty Rose**: `#d4a5a5` - Soft primary color
- **Clay**: `#b87d6d` - Earthy accent
- **Sand**: `#e8d5c4` - Warm neutral backgrounds
- **Deep Burgundy**: `#5d2e46` - Rich dark contrast

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Fashion decks, beauty-brand materials, wedding planning content, interior design portfolios, boutique business pitches.
