# Forest Canopy

An organic, grounded theme drawing on earthy tones inspired by the depth of dense forest environments.

## Color Palette

- **Forest Green**: `#2d4a2b` - Primary dark green
- **Sage**: `#7d8471` - Muted green accent
- **Olive**: `#a4ac86` - Light accent color
- **Ivory**: `#faf9f6` - Backgrounds and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSerif Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Environmental and sustainability presentations, outdoor brand materials, wellness content, organic product showcases.
