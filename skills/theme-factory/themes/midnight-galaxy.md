# Midnight Galaxy

A striking cosmic theme built on deep purples and enigmatic tones designed to make a strong visual impact in presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Deep Purple**: `#2b1e3e` - Rich dark base
- **Cosmic Blue**: `#4a4e8f` - Mystical mid-tone
- **Lavender**: `#a490c2` - Soft accent color
- **Silver**: `#e6e6fa` - Light highlights and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Entertainment-industry decks, gaming presentations, nightlife venue materials, luxury brand content, creative agency pitches.
