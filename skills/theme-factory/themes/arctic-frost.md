# Arctic Frost

A crisp, cool theme drawn from winter aesthetics that communicates clarity, precision, and professionalism.

## Color Palette

- **Ice Blue**: `#d4e4f7` - Light backgrounds and highlights
- **Steel Blue**: `#4a6fa5` - Primary accent color
- **Silver**: `#c0c0c0` - Metallic accent elements
- **Crisp White**: `#fafafa` - Clean backgrounds and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Healthcare presentations, technology solutions, winter sports, clean tech, and pharmaceutical content.
