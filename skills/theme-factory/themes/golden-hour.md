# Golden Hour

A deep, warm autumnal palette that establishes an inviting and refined atmosphere throughout a presentation.

## Color Palette

- **Mustard Yellow**: `#f4a900` - Bold primary accent
- **Terracotta**: `#c1666b` - Warm secondary color
- **Warm Beige**: `#d4b896` - Neutral backgrounds
- **Chocolate Brown**: `#4a403a` - Dark text and anchors

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Restaurant decks, hospitality brand materials, autumn campaigns, cozy lifestyle content, artisan product presentations.
