Question me thoroughly on every element of this plan until we arrive at a mutual understanding. Move through each branch of the design tree in sequence, settling the dependencies between decisions one at a time. For each question, offer your recommended answer.

Pose questions individually, waiting for a response before moving to the next one. Firing multiple questions simultaneously is disorienting.

If a question can be resolved by examining the codebase, consult the codebase instead of asking.
