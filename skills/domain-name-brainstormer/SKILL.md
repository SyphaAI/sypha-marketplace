# Domain Name Brainstormer

This skill helps you identify the ideal domain name for your project by generating creative options and confirming what is actually available to register.

## When to Use This Skill

- Starting a new project or company
- Launching a product or service
- Building a personal brand or portfolio site
- Rebranding an existing project
- Registering a domain for a side project
- Finding available alternatives when your first choice is taken

## What This Skill Does

1. **Understands Your Project**: Examines what you are building and who it is for
2. **Generates Creative Names**: Produces relevant, memorable domain options
3. **Checks Availability**: Confirms which domains are available across multiple TLDs
4. **Multiple Extensions**: Suggests .com, .io, .dev, .ai, .app, and more
5. **Provides Alternatives**: Offers variations when top choices are taken
6. **Branding Insights**: Explains why certain names are effective

## Domain Naming Tips

### What Makes a Good Domain

✓ **Short**: Ideally under 15 characters
✓ **Memorable**: Easy to recall and spell
✓ **Pronounceable**: Can be spoken in conversation
✓ **Descriptive**: Hints at what you do
✓ **Brandable**: Distinct enough to stand out
✓ **No hyphens**: Easier to share verbally

### TLD Guide

- **.com**: Universal, trusted, great for businesses
- **.io**: Tech startups, developer tools
- **.dev**: Developer-focused products
- **.ai**: AI/ML products
- **.app**: Mobile or web applications
- **.co**: Alternative to .com
- **.xyz**: Modern, creative projects
- **.design**: Creative/design agencies
- **.tech**: Technology companies

## Example Workflows

### Startup Launch
1. Describe your startup idea
2. Receive 10-15 domain suggestions across TLDs
3. Review availability and pricing
4. Select your top 3 favorites
5. Register immediately

### Personal Brand
1. Share your name and profession
2. Receive variations (firstname.com, firstnamelastname.dev, etc.)
3. Also check social media handle availability
4. Register a consistent brand across platforms

### Product Naming
1. Describe the product and target market
2. Receive creative, brandable names
3. Check for trademark conflicts
4. Verify domain and social availability
5. Test names with the target audience

## Tips for Success

1. **Act Fast**: Good domains are claimed quickly
2. **Register Variations**: Secure both .com and .io to protect the brand
3. **Avoid Numbers**: Difficult to convey verbally
4. **Check Social Media**: Confirm that @username is also available
5. **Say It Out Loud**: Verify it is easy to pronounce
6. **Check Trademarks**: Confirm there are no legal conflicts
7. **Think Long-term**: Will it still be relevant in 5 years?

## Pricing Context

When suggesting domains, I'll note:
- Standard domains: ~$10-15/year
- Premium TLDs (.io, .ai): ~$30-50/year
- Taken domains: Market price if listed
- Premium domains: $hundreds to $thousands
